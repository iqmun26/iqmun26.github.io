The Basics

How a MUN Works

✦

Model United Nations is a simulation of the real UN. Each student becomes a delegate, represents a country, and works with others to debate a global issue and write a resolution that can win a vote.

Get Assigned

You receive a committee and a country. You speak for that country, not for yourself.

Research

Study your agenda and your country's real policy. Write a short position paper.

Debate

Give speeches from the speakers' list, raise points, and answer questions. Chairs keep order.

Caucus & Negotiate

Debate pauses so delegates can form blocs, trade ideas, and draft together.

Resolve

Blocs submit draft resolutions, amend them, and the committee votes.

Present Yourself

Dress Code

✦

Casual is welcome. Just keep it neat, modest and presentable.

Befitting

  • Clean, neat everyday clothes
  • Shirts, polos, kurtas and tops
  • Jeans or trousers
  • Dresses and skirts at a modest length
  • Comfortable shoes

Not Befitting

  • Shorts, for everyone
  • Revealing or very tight clothing
  • Torn or ripped clothing
  • Pajamas or gym wear
  • Offensive slogans or graphics
